# Uizard

> Uizard is an AI-powered UI design and prototyping platform that lets non-designers turn text prompts, screenshots, and hand-drawn sketches into editable mockups and multi-screen prototypes. Founded in Copenhagen out of the pix2code machine-learning research project, Uizard joined Miro in May 2024 and continues to operate as its own product. Uizard publishes no public API, developer portal, or client SDKs.

Key AI features: Autodesigner 2.0 (text-to-multi-screen prototypes), Design Assistant, Screenshot Scanner (screenshots to editable mockups), Wireframe Scanner (sketches to digital designs), Design Review, and theme generation. A Figma-to-Uizard plugin imports Figma components and designs.
